2. Description of Service

Avali Live Link monitors configured Twitch channels and sends livestream notifications to designated Discord channels when streamers go live.

Features include:

  • Automated Twitch livestream notifications.
  • Support for multiple Twitch streamers per Discord server.
  • Customizable livestream announcement messages.
  • Configurable Discord notification channels.
  • Twitch profile pictures and livestream previews.
  • Duplicate stream notification protection.
  • An 8-hour notification cooldown.
  • Administrative slash commands.
  • Test notifications.

7. Livestream Notifications and Cooldowns

Avali Live Link periodically checks configured Twitch channels for livestream activity.

The Bot currently checks for livestream updates approximately every 30 seconds while the Service is running.

The Bot uses stream identification to reduce duplicate notifications.

An 8-hour cooldown applies to each configured Twitch streamer within each Discord server.

During the cooldown period, additional livestream sessions may not trigger another announcement.

Notifications are not guaranteed for every livestream session.
