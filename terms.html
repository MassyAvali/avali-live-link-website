2. Description of Service

Avali Live Link monitors configured Twitch channels and sends notifications to designated Discord channels when streamers go live.

Features may include:

  • Automated Twitch livestream notifications.
  • Support for multiple streamers per Discord server.
  • Custom announcement messages.
  • Twitch profile pictures and stream previews.
  • Duplicate notification prevention.
  • An 8-hour notification cooldown.
  • Administrative slash commands.
  • Test notifications.
