💜 Privacy Policy

Avali Live Link

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Avali Live Link ("the Bot", "we", "our") is a Discord application that provides automated Twitch livestream notifications to Discord communities.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Avali Live Link collects, processes, stores, and uses to provide its services.

By using Avali Live Link, you acknowledge the data practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Avali Live Link stores limited information necessary to operate its Twitch notification features.

This information includes:

  • Discord server IDs.
  • Discord notification channel IDs.
  • Twitch usernames configured by server administrators.
  • Custom livestream announcement messages.
  • Twitch stream IDs used for duplicate detection.
  • Notification timestamps used for the 8-hour cooldown.

The Bot may temporarily process Discord interaction information, such as command options and the identity of the user issuing a command, to respond to requests and check permissions.

Avali Live Link does not require users to provide passwords, payment information, or personal Discord messages.

The Bot is not designed to collect or monitor ordinary Discord conversations.

3. How We Use Information

Collected and stored information is used exclusively to operate, maintain, and improve the Bot's functionality.

  • Monitor configured Twitch channels.
  • Deliver livestream announcements to Discord.
  • Maintain server-specific notification settings.
  • Display custom announcement messages.
  • Prevent duplicate livestream notifications.
  • Enforce an 8-hour notification cooldown.
  • Respond to administrative slash commands.
  • Diagnose technical issues and maintain service reliability.

4. Third-Party Services

Avali Live Link uses Discord and Twitch to provide its notification services.

The Twitch API is used to retrieve publicly available livestream information, including streamer usernames, livestream status, game categories, stream previews, and profile images.

Discord is used to receive application commands and deliver notifications to configured server channels.

These third-party platforms operate under their own privacy policies and terms of service.

Discord Privacy Policy: https://discord.com/privacy

Twitch Privacy Notice: Twitch Privacy Notice

5. Data Storage and Retention

Avali Live Link stores configuration information in a local database maintained by the Bot operator.

Configuration information is retained while the relevant Twitch notification configuration remains active or until an authorized deletion request is processed.

The Bot does not sell stored information to advertisers or other third parties.

Information is not used for targeted advertising or commercial profiling.

Technical logs and database backups, if maintained, may have separate retention periods. Users may contact the Bot operator for information about applicable retention practices.

6. Data Deletion and User Rights

Discord server administrators with the required permissions may remove Twitch notification configurations directly through the Bot.

  • /twitch clear - Removes one configured Twitch streamer from the current Discord server.
  • /twitch clearall - Removes all Twitch notification configurations associated with the current Discord server.

These commands delete the relevant configuration records, including saved channel settings, custom messages, stream IDs, and cooldown timestamps, from the active database.

Users and server administrators may request access to, correction of, or deletion of applicable stored information by contacting:

massgamingavali@outlook.com

Please include "Avali Live Link Privacy Request" in the email subject and describe your request. Additional information may be requested to verify authorization before changes are made to server configurations.

Requests will be reviewed and handled in accordance with applicable laws and operational requirements.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to help protect stored configuration information and application credentials.

Access to the Bot's database and authentication credentials is intended to be limited to authorized operators.

However, no method of electronic storage or internet communication can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Avali Live Link is intended for use in accordance with the applicable age requirements established by Discord and Twitch.

The Bot is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe information has been collected improperly, please contact the Bot operator for review.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in the Bot's functionality, data practices, security requirements, or applicable laws.

Any updated policy will be published on this page.

The effective date will be revised when material changes are made.

10. Contact Information

For questions about this Privacy Policy, personal information, data deletion, or Avali Live Link's privacy practices, please contact the Bot operator.
